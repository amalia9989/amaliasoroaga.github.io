Sustainable
Sports Events

Read the case study

The visual identity of a free web portal that helps organizers make sporting events more sustainable – from club tournaments to world championships.

Client:  DOSB · via econcess
Online:  January 2025
Role:  Art Direction

01 · Challenge

Complex content, clear design

“Nachhaltige Sport[groß]veranstaltungen” was a two-year research project by the German Olympic Sports Confederation (DOSB), the German Sport University Cologne and Öko-Institut. Its results – over 600 measures and 268 goals – had to become a portal that beginners and experts can both use.

The design needed to make a lot of complex content feel simple, and be accessible from the start.

02 · My role

Art direction at econcess

I was responsible for the creative side of the project: the web design of the portal, the graphic design and the design guidelines that the team keeps building with. The development was done by the econcess dev team.

03 · Design guidelines

One system for every page

The guidelines define all content elements of the website – entry tiles, cards, accordions, text with icons, downloads, news and good-practice pages – for desktop, tablet and mobile.

They also set the typography, the color palette with the SDG accent colors, and the buttons, so new content always looks consistent.

04 · Icon concept

Icons that show the structure

Each content area got its own color from the SDG palette: red for transformation areas, orange for steering areas, blue for fields of action and yellow for goals and indicators.

Every icon exists in three variants – symbol only, outlined circle and filled circle – with the line weight adjusted from 1 pt to 2 pt so it stays readable on color.

05 · Result

Online and award-winning

The portal has been live since January 2025, in German and English. The research project and the portal won the Humboldt Prize for Sustainability 2025 (1st place, Transfer) and the Sport Positive Award 2026 in the category “Inspired Innovation”.
